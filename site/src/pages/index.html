How AIR works

From log file to verifiable record, deterministically.

No machine-learning verdicts. Every step is a rule you can read, with a threshold that cites its source. The same log produces byte-identical analysis every time.

  1. Ingest

    A PX4 flight log and your written procedures as a machine-readable ruleset.

  2. Normalize

    Firmware layouts drift across versions — topics and units are aliased into one normalized schema.

  3. Reconstruct

    One synchronized timeline: telemetry series and discrete events on a common clock, UTC-anchored when GPS time allows.

  4. Analyze

    Deterministic analyzers for battery, GPS quality, estimator health, vibration, and log integrity — each finding carries its threshold and basis.

  5. Cross-check

    Independent sensors are compared; disagreements become findings that quote both sources. Agreement is stated too.

  6. Check procedures

    Your rules, evaluated over the flight window: PASS, FAIL, or NOT EVALUABLE — missing data is never a silent pass.

  7. Sign

    The record and source-file digests are committed to an RFC 9162 Merkle tree; the root is Ed25519-signed, with an optional RFC 3161 timestamp.

  8. Verify

    A standalone ~200-line verifier checks the bundle offline — built to be read by the other side's expert, no network, no trust in any vendor cloud.

$ python3 verify.py --fingerprint <operator's published fingerprint>
VERIFICATION PASSED — record is intact and signed by the trusted key. (exit 0)
$ python3 verify.py
VERIFICATION INCOMPLETE — integrity OK, signer NOT verified (exit 3)

Change one byte of any finding, of the HTML page, or of a source file and verification fails — we test exactly that. The key inside the bundle is not trusted on its own: without the operator's published fingerprint the verifier says the signer is unverified. What this proves: the record hasn't changed since it was signed by that key, and the source files match their digests. What it deliberately does not prove: who controls the key, that the analysis is true, or anything legal. Cryptography seals the record; it doesn't decide the case.

Read before quoting these numbers

  • The corpus index contains 436,637 logs. We evaluated 100. Nothing here extends beyond them.
  • Labels are uploader-assigned and noisy — one "crash"-labeled log in our fixtures turned out to be a bench test that never left the ground.
  • Round 2 of the evaluation followed an ingestion-coverage fix discovered in round 1 (an arming-topic aliasing gap on older firmware). Both rounds and the methodology are published in the repository.
  • A fresh-seed confirmation run has not yet been performed. Until it is, treat these as internal results.
  • The 20 MB size cap biases the sample toward shorter flights — a known, disclosed bias.

Limitations

What AIR can't do yet — stated before you ask.

An incident-record tool that oversells itself defeats its own purpose. These are the current boundaries, kept current in the repository.

FORMATS

PX4 .ulg only today

ArduPilot dataflash and DJI logs are not implemented. They're the roadmap's top items — DJI additionally depends on a revocable vendor key.

PROCEDURES

Six quantitative rule types

Altitude, distance, speed, GPS count, landing battery, forbidden modes. Real ops manuals also contain weather minima, airspace, and crew rules AIR can't express yet.

ALTITUDE

Height above launch, not terrain AGL

Altitude rules use height above the home (launch) position, not terrain-corrected AGL; over sloping terrain the two differ. Without a logged home position the rule is NOT EVALUABLE — barometric altitude is never substituted.

CRYPTOGRAPHY

Integrity, not truth

The signature shows the record hasn't changed and sources match digests. It does not prove who controls the signing key, analysis correctness, legal compliance, or admissibility. AIR's records are meant to support a qualified person's certification; AIR does not certify identity or admissibility itself.

EVALUATION

100 processed logs

Public evaluation covers a 100-log seeded sample, not the 436,637-log corpus, and a fresh-seed confirmation run is still owed.

SCOPE

Single-source evidence today

Weather, Remote ID, and detection-alert ingest are roadmap, not product. Today's records are built from the flight log plus your procedures.

JUDGMENT

Not an investigator

AIR is not a guaranteed crash detector and not a replacement for a trained investigator. It prepares the evidence a human decides with.

STAGE

No customers yet

AIR is pre-revenue with no customers, partnerships, or regulatory approval. That's why the ask below is a conversation, not a contract.
