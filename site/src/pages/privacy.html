Privacy

Effective 2026-07-28 · applies to this website (air.findanything.dev)

The short version

This website is a static page. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, embeds no third-party scripts or fonts, and makes no requests to any server other than the one serving these files. There is no account system, no upload feature, and no database.

What we receive

Nothing, by default. The site cannot collect anything: there is no form that submits to a server. The "Request a demo" form only composes an email in your own mail client — until you press send in your mail app, nothing leaves your machine, and we have no way of knowing you opened the form.

If you email us (hello@findanything.dev or security@findanything.dev), we receive what you send. Those addresses are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to a personal mailbox. We use your message to reply to you, we don't add you to any list, and we don't share or sell correspondence. Ask at any time and we'll delete the thread.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any host, Cloudflare processes connection metadata (such as IP addresses) at the infrastructure level to deliver the site and may keep operational logs under its own privacy policy. We do not receive, view, or use per-visitor logs.

The AIR software is separate

The analysis software shown on this site runs locally on an operator's own machine and is not part of this website. This site has no ability to receive flight logs.

Changes

If our practices ever change, this page will change first, with a new effective date.
